Who We Are

A Church Built on Faith & Fire

Pure-Grace Revival Ministries is a dynamic, Spirit-filled church committed to preaching the authentic gospel of Jesus Christ. We believe in the power of worship, the truth of the Word, and the transforming grace of God available to every soul.

  • Uncompromising Preaching of the Word
  • Spirit-led Worship & Intercession
  • Community Outreach & Evangelism
  • Discipleship & Leadership Development
  • Prayer — the Heartbeat of Our Ministry

Our Vision

To raise a generation of believers who walk in the fullness of God's grace, carrying revival to every nation — Christ to the Nations.
